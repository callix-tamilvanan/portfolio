About

I started my career as an Audio Engineer in 2019, working on television shows for Vijay TV, Sun TV, Zee Thamizh, and Puthiya Thalaimurai through Pro Media MJR Lights and Sounds.

When the COVID lockdowns shut down production in 2020, I pivoted into Assistant Direction — first on Raj TV's Pongal special, then Captain TV's Kolaru Kudumbam, and eventually Makkal TV's Punnagai Mannargal.

I moved into Associate Direction and then, by 2024, into Cinematography. Alongside my TV work, I've been a freelance photographer since 2019, focusing on candid photography. I also work in event management — coordinating DJ performances and sound system setups — and I do pyrotechnics for stage shows.

I'm a fast learner who adapts quickly to any situation, and I'm comfortable leading and managing a team.
