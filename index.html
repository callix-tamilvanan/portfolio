About

I started my career as an Audio Engineer in 2019, working on Tamil television shows for Vijay TV, Sun TV, Zee Thamizh, and Puthiya Thalaimurai through Pro Media MJR Lights and Sounds.

When COVID lockdowns shut down production, I pivoted to Assistant Direction — first on Raj TV's Pongal special, then Captain TV's Kolaru Kudumbam. In 2023 I joined Makkal TV's Punnagai Mannargal as Assistant Director, moved into Associate Direction for two months, and by 2024 had grown into the role of Cinematographer on the same production.

Alongside television, I've worked as a freelance photographer since 2019, with a focus on candid photography. I also work in event management — coordinating DJ performances and sound system setups — and I do pyrotechnics for stage shows.

I'm a fast learner who adapts quickly to any situation, and I'm comfortable leading and managing a team. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Visual Communication (BSc Viscom, 2017–2020).

I'm currently building Black Pulse — a free and open-source music player for Android.
